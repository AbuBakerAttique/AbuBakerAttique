## 🚀 Featured Projects

> ### 🎯 [AI Interview Coach](https://github.com/AbuBakerAttique/AI-Interview-Coach) &nbsp;·&nbsp; Real-Time Multi-Modal Analysis &nbsp; `2026`
> A production-grade AI system evaluating interview performance in real time using **4 deep learning models trained from scratch**, fused into a single confidence score with actionable feedback.
>
> | Model | Dataset | Architecture | Accuracy |
> |---|---|---|:---:|
> | Speech Confidence | RAVDESS (2,880 clips) | CNN + LSTM (MFCC) | **93.40%** |
> | Facial Expression | FER2013 (28,821 imgs) | Custom CNN | **75.54%** |
> | Posture Scorer | MediaPipe landmarks (4,794) | Neural Network | **97.29%** |
> | Eye Contact | OpenCV Haar Cascade | Rule-based | — |
>
> **Stack:** `Python` `PyTorch` `MediaPipe` `OpenCV` `Librosa` `Flask` `React`

>
> **Stack:** `Python` `MediaPipe` `NumPy` `Three.js` `Web Speech API`
